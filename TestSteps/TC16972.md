# TC16972 - TaskKPI_WhenLabelIsRendered_ShouldUseKPIsCapitalization

Release ORION 2025.2000.1 - RT:27053 (KPI ribbon and KPI configuration).

Categories: Regression, KPI, Configuration, Group, Label, Administration

Setup (Setups/TC16972.cs): creates the group the test configures its KPIs on (Orion Worker Portal DLL: Groups.CreateGroup, read-or-create by name). The KPI configuration is stored per group, so every test works on its own group and the logged in user is assigned to it by the test.

Steps in the test:

1. Log in to Orion.
2. Go to Administration > Groups, search for the group created by the setup and open it.
3. Verify the tab is labelled "Group KPIs".
4. Open the Group KPIs tab. Verify the headings are "Claims Processing KPIs" and "Customer Service KPIs".
5. Verify the text of the page (as written, not as the CSS capitalizes it) never spells the word Kpi, Kpis, kpi or kpis.
6. Open Administration > KPI Settings and verify the dialog title "KPI Settings" and its "KPI Refresh Interval" label use the same capitalization; close the dialog.

Notes:
- The test cases are named "TaskKPI"; in the build under test the KPI feature has Claims Processing KPIs (Claims Queue ribbon) and Customer Service KPIs (Tickets ribbon), and My Tasks has no KPI ribbon. "Task" KPIs are therefore the Customer Service KPIs and "My Task" is the Tickets page.
- Shared helpers: utils/kpi.ts. After the test the user is taken out of the group again (afterEach), so the next test's ribbon only shows its own KPIs.

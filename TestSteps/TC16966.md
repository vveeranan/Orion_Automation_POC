# TC16966 - TaskKPI_WhenAuthorizedUserOpensConfiguration_ShouldShowTaskKPIs

Release ORION 2025.2000.1 - RT:27053 (KPI ribbon and KPI configuration).

Categories: Regression, KPI, Configuration, Group, CustomerService, Administration

Setup (Setups/TC16966.cs): creates the group the test configures its KPIs on (Orion Worker Portal DLL: Groups.CreateGroup, read-or-create by name). The KPI configuration is stored per group, so every test works on its own group and the logged in user is assigned to it by the test.

Steps in the test:

1. Log in to Orion.
2. Go to Administration > Groups, search for the group created by the setup and open it.
3. Verify the group page offers the Group Assignments, Group Flags and Group KPIs tabs.
4. Open the Group KPIs tab. Verify the Claims Processing KPIs and Customer Service KPIs headings are shown.
5. Verify all four Customer Service ("Task") KPIs are listed - Resolution Time, Tickets Created, Tickets Resolved, Tickets Close Rate - and none is enabled yet (each shows Enable).
6. Verify the editor panel asks the user to "Select a KPI to configure it."

Notes:
- The test cases are named "TaskKPI"; in the build under test the KPI feature has Claims Processing KPIs (Claims Queue ribbon) and Customer Service KPIs (Tickets ribbon), and My Tasks has no KPI ribbon. "Task" KPIs are therefore the Customer Service KPIs and "My Task" is the Tickets page.
- Shared helpers: utils/kpi.ts. After the test the user is taken out of the group again (afterEach), so the next test's ribbon only shows its own KPIs.

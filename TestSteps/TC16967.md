# TC16967 - TaskKPI_WhenUserSelectsTaskKPIs_ShouldShowTaskDefinitions

Release ORION 2025.2000.1 - RT:27053 (KPI ribbon and KPI configuration).

Categories: Regression, KPI, Configuration, Group, CustomerService, Administration

Setup (Setups/TC16967.cs): creates the group the test configures its KPIs on (Orion Worker Portal DLL: Groups.CreateGroup, read-or-create by name). The KPI configuration is stored per group, so every test works on its own group and the logged in user is assigned to it by the test.

Steps in the test:

1. Log in to Orion.
2. Go to Administration > Groups, search for the group created by the setup and open it.
3. Open the Group KPIs tab.
4. For each Customer Service KPI: click Enable, click the KPI name, and verify the KPI editor shows the KPI name, a description, the Benchmark and Time Period fields and the Three Bar Graph / Ticker display types.
5. Select Tickets Created and verify its description is "The number of tickets created for a configured period of time."

Notes:
- The test cases are named "TaskKPI"; in the build under test the KPI feature has Claims Processing KPIs (Claims Queue ribbon) and Customer Service KPIs (Tickets ribbon), and My Tasks has no KPI ribbon. "Task" KPIs are therefore the Customer Service KPIs and "My Task" is the Tickets page.
- Shared helpers: utils/kpi.ts. After the test the user is taken out of the group again (afterEach), so the next test's ribbon only shows its own KPIs.

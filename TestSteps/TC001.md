# TC001 - Create member call ticket

1. Log in to Orion.
2. Go to Customer Service > My Tickets and note the ticket count.
3. Click Add Ticket, then click Member Call. A new ticket is created.
4. Enter the caller and caller phone.
5. Search for the member and select the match.
6. Go back to My Tickets and verify the new ticket is listed with the count up by one.

# TC16986 - TaskKPI_WhenClaimsQueueLoads_ShouldPreserveExistingKPIs

Release ORION 2025.2000.1 - RT:27053 (KPI ribbon and KPI configuration).

Categories: Regression, KPI, Ribbon, Existing, Claims, Queue

Setup (Setups/TC16986.cs): creates the group the test configures its KPIs on (Orion Worker Portal DLL: Groups.CreateGroup, read-or-create by name). The KPI configuration is stored per group, so every test works on its own group and the logged in user is assigned to it by the test.

Steps in the test:

1. Log in to Orion.
2. Go to Administration > Groups, search for the group created by the setup and open it.
3. On Group Assignments click the logged in user in the Users list to assign them to the group (a clean-up step takes them out again after the test).
4. Open the Group KPIs tab.
5. Enable Claims Pending if needed, select it and set Benchmark Less, the benchmark value, Time Period Current Day, Display Type Three Bar Graph (each change is saved straight away).
6. Go to Claim Inventory Management > My Claims Queue and verify the "Claims Queue" page loads and its grid lists queue items.
7. Verify the ribbon shows one three-bar-graph tile.
8. Click the tile and verify the dialog is for Claims Pending: Benchmark "<= <value>", Style "Bar Chart" (the dialog's name for the Three Bar Graph display type); close the dialog.

Notes:
- The test cases are named "TaskKPI"; in the build under test the KPI feature has Claims Processing KPIs (Claims Queue ribbon) and Customer Service KPIs (Tickets ribbon), and My Tasks has no KPI ribbon. "Task" KPIs are therefore the Customer Service KPIs and "My Task" is the Tickets page.
- Shared helpers: utils/kpi.ts. After the test the user is taken out of the group again (afterEach), so the next test's ribbon only shows its own KPIs.

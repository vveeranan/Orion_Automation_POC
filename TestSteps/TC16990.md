# TC16990 - TaskKPI_WhenExistingKPISettingsOpen_ShouldPreserveRefreshOptions

Release ORION 2025.2000.1 - RT:27053 (KPI ribbon and KPI configuration).

Categories: Regression, KPI, Settings, Existing, Administration

Setup (Setups/TC16990.cs): creates the group the test configures its KPIs on (Orion Worker Portal DLL: Groups.CreateGroup, read-or-create by name). The KPI configuration is stored per group, so every test works on its own group and the logged in user is assigned to it by the test.

Steps in the test:

1. Log in to Orion.
2. Open Administration > KPI Settings and verify the dialog offers the refresh intervals 20 sec, 60 sec and 5 min with 60 sec selected (the default); close it.
3. Open it again, choose 5 min and close it (saved on close). Open it once more and verify 5 min is now selected and 60 sec is not.
4. Set it back to 60 sec and verify that was kept.

Notes:
- The test cases are named "TaskKPI"; in the build under test the KPI feature has Claims Processing KPIs (Claims Queue ribbon) and Customer Service KPIs (Tickets ribbon), and My Tasks has no KPI ribbon. "Task" KPIs are therefore the Customer Service KPIs and "My Task" is the Tickets page.
- Shared helpers: utils/kpi.ts. After the test the user is taken out of the group again (afterEach), so the next test's ribbon only shows its own KPIs.
